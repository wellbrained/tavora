# Theme starter

A theme to copy and make your own. It is the Tavora theme, Dark and Light, under its own id.

1. Copy this folder into Tavora's themes folder. In Tavora, Settings > Appearance > **Open folder** shows where that is. **New theme** does the copying for you.
2. Change `id`, `name` and the colours in `tavora.theme.json`. Every colour is `#rrggbb`.
3. Press **Reload** in Settings > Appearance and pick your theme.

If a colour pair doesn't have enough contrast, Tavora doesn't load the theme and Appearance names the pair. The format and the contrast rules are in the [theme reference](../../apps/docs/reference/theme.md).
