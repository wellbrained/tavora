# Browser asks, also when you change a site

- While the Browser asks to open another site, changing a site's sign-in or ad blocking leaves the question in place. Only Open opens it.
- Its sign-in and ad-block buttons rest while it asks.
- When you sign in to a site for all workspaces, or stop, a page you opened moves along as before. One that opened by itself, or that the Browser reopened, asks first.
