# A tidier Settings

- Settings has a shorter menu that fits without scrolling: General, Appearance, Games, Shortcuts, Modules, Connections, Overlays & streaming, Browser and Developer, with About & updates at the bottom.
- Games has what happens when a game starts or closes, your library (with Workspaces and Trash), the Steam import and the online features.
- Connections lists everything your modules may reach: websites, Discord, AI accounts, system info, media and leaderboards. Open a row to see which modules use it and take access back.
- Each setting says in one short line what it does. Rest the pointer on a dotted name for more.
- Settings you haven't seen yet say New.
- Click your picture in the rail for your profile: name, initials and ranks, plus your account, the cloud copy and messages.
