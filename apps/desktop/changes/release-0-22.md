# A new Home, the Overlay page and Community modules

- A new Home: search from it, continue the game you played last, and see what happened while you were away.
- Overlays have their own page, opened from the rail. Set them up for all games or one game, press Start or Stop, and their panels run without opening a workspace.
- Every item on the rail is a workspace, for a game or just for you. Messages, module updates and the Overlay page sit in the rail too.
- Community modules: modules other people made, with ratings, reviews and Contact the developer.
- Leaderboards in modules, with your ranks in your profile. Nothing goes public until you say yes, and you can hide your ranks.
- Module settings open big in one look, also from a panel over your game. To-Dos goes first: pick font, size, colours and more.
- To-Dos: Enter starts the next to-do, the trash button deletes one in a click, and Keep finished to-dos is a switch.
- Whiteboard: note cards, pictures with captions, lines and arrows between cards, board templates and a grid.
- Calculator: save results as values, round up, short numbers like 1.5M, and a ⚙ menu with options.
- System Monitor: the RAM below the apps list adds up.
- New games start with Notes and To-Dos, and a module you add opens where you work.
- A fresh look for notifications, menus with icons, and tooltips next to what you point at.
- Ctrl+K finds your own things first.
- When a module needs your OK, it asks in its own panel.
- Clearer messages when you're offline or something fails, in English and German.
