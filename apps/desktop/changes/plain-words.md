# Plainer words

- To-Dos has a "Keep finished to-dos" toggle instead of "Done: keep".
- The Calculator's k, m, b and ⌈x⌉ keys say what they do when you point at them.
- Community modules show New only for two weeks, hide tiny install counts, call early versions Preview and say where a module works.
- Permission prompts lead with what a module wants, like "Let System Monitor see how busy your PC is?", on a lighter backdrop in light themes.
- Settings > Development stays tucked away until you say you want to make modules.
- The profile card saves as you type and links to your account.
- German panels say "Lade Notizen…" while they load.
- The expanded rail says Home at the top.
