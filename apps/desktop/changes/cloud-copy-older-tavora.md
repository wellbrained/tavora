# Cloud copy fixes

- Opened an older Tavora during a restore? Your data comes back, and Tavora asks which side to keep.
- Tavora tells you where your data from before a restore is saved, and keeps that backup.
- While Tavora asks about data it couldn't open, an older Tavora leaves your cloud copy alone.
- Going back from a damaged restore puts your pictures and module data back as they were.
