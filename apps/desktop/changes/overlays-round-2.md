# Overlays and panels in your game

- One key, Tavora over game, opens your overlays and panels over the game. Set it in Settings > Overlays.
- A dark veil covers the game while it's open. Move, resize or close your overlays and use your panels. Press the key again or Esc when you're done.
- Panels that support it get Show in game in their menu. They appear only while the layer is open.
- Overlays made outside a game, like in Personal, show over every game.
- A Live badge on a panel's tab shows what's in your game. Click it to see it in Settings.
- Preview shows an overlay in a small window, on a dark, light or checkered background. Find it in Settings > Overlays and Settings > Streaming.
- Hide all for now in Settings > Overlays hides everything over your game until you show it again.
- The arrange keys and Show over game for every panel are gone.
