# Whiteboard vocabulary

- **Board** — A named, independent space for drawings, notes and pictures.
- **Card** — A note or captioned picture whose parts move together.
- **Connector** — A line joining objects, with no arrowhead, one arrowhead or arrowheads at both ends.
- **Starter board** — A new board prepared for a particular kind of work.
- **Grid** — An optional guide for lining up objects on one board.
