Plan production lines and look things up while you play.

- **Planner:** say what you want to make per minute. You see the machines, power and raw resources it takes, and can pick other recipes.
- See your factory as a chain, a graph or a list, and keep as many plans as you like.
- Tick the alternate recipes you've unlocked in your world.
- **Codex:** look up items, recipes and buildings, and plan any item with one click.
- Works offline.
